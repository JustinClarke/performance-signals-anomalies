Why Tukey after ANOVA

ANOVA only says some means differ. Tukey says which pairs, holding the error rate at 5% across all six comparisons. Grades are bounded, so Kruskal-Wallis checks we aren't leaning on normality.

Act 1A suspicious detail

But the students who study more were already different

Why we can't stop at ANOVA

The raw gap mixes studying with who studies: more girls, fewer past failures, more ambition. Leave those out and their effect is credited to study time. That's omitted-variable bias, so next we compare like with like using multiple regression.
Confounding · omitted-variable bias
Share of girls, mean number of past class failures, and share who want higher education, by study-time bracket (Portuguese course, n = 649).04

Fair warningWhat this case cannot prove

These are associations from two schools, and we chose the story after looking

Not causal

Study time is self-chosen, self-reported and coarse (4 levels). It is likely endogenous, and there's no instrument in the data.

G1, G2 are bad controls

They are partly outcomes of studying, so M4 understates effort. The R² of 0.86 is partly mechanical.

The dropout reading is ours

The data never say why G3 = 0. Without them, the absence effect describes students who completed the course.

Maths is not independent

A second course, mostly the same students. It is a consistency check, not a fresh sample.

Forking paths

The story was picked after pilot checks, and we ran about 50 tests without multiplicity correction. Results near p = 0.05 stay in backup, marked fragile.

Narrow sample

Two Portuguese secondary schools in 2005-06; only 35 students in the top study bracket.
