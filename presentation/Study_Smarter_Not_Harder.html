What we can't claim

Associations, not causes: five limits on what this shows

  1. Observational, not causal.Study time is self-chosen and likely endogenous (reverse causality, omitted motivation). No instrument.
  2. G1 and G2 are "bad controls" for effort.They partly result from studying, so M4 understates the effect of study time.
  3. "Apparent dropouts" is our reading.The data never say why a final grade is 0. Every key result is shown with and without them.
  4. Coarse measurement.4-level self-reported study time; the bounded 0-20 scale likely explains the RESET flag.
  5. Narrow sample.Two Portuguese schools in 2005-06; only 35 students in the top study group.
